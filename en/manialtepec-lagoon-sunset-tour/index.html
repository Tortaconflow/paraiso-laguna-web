Manialtepec Lagoon Sunset Tour: Mangroves, Beach Bonfire & Glow

Experience daylight wildlife melt seamlessly into starlight over 5 peaceful hours. Cruise 13 km of mangrove canals watching coastal birds, step ashore onto untouched Puerto Suelo Beach for a sunset bonfire, and return across glowing bioluminescence.

Three Magical Moments in One Unhurried Outing

Designed for travelers seeking more than a quick night boat ride, our Manialtepec Lagoon Sunset & Bonfire Tour reveals the entire afternoon-to-night transformation over five hours. Launching at 3:00 PM gives bright daylight across 13 km of scenic mangrove channels filled with herons, kingfishers, and brown pelicans.

Arriving at semi-virgin Playa Puerto Suelo, we light a warm beach bonfire to toast marshmallows as Pacific waves glow amber before navigating back through shimmering bioluminescent waters.

What Is Included?

  • Roundtrip hotel transport from Puerto Escondido.
  • Guided boat cruise with certified life jackets and native captain.
  • Beach bonfire stop at Playa Puerto Suelo with sunset marshmallows + bioluminescence finale.
  • Traveler insurance included throughout the excursion.

🔥 Why This Combo Tour Stands Out

  • Combines daytime birdwatching, mangrove exploration, beach bonfire, and night glow.
  • Slower golden-hour pace favorite among couples, photographers, and small groups.
  • Natural transition from sunset ocean breeze to dark-sky lagoon stars.

Afternoon-to-Night Itinerary (5 Hours)

  1. 3:00 PM — Hotel pickup in Puerto Escondido and short van transfer to Manialtepec.
  2. 3:30 PM — Board your boat and cruise 13 km of mangrove islands watching coastal birdlife.
  3. 5:00 PM — Arrive at peaceful Playa Puerto Suelo where the river meets the Pacific Ocean.
  4. 6:15 PM — Watch sunset by a cozy beach bonfire roasting marshmallows under emerging stars.
  5. 7:15 PM — Glide back across bioluminescent waters before hotel drop-off (~8:00 PM).

What to Bring

  • Light daytime clothing plus a thin windbreaker or long sleeve for the evening boat breeze.
  • Swimsuit and towel if you plan to dip into the glowing lagoon on the return leg.
  • Camera, sunglasses, and chemical-free biodegradable repellent.

Frequently Asked Questions About Lagoon Sunset & Bonfire

Does this sunset tour finish with bioluminescence?

Yes. On the evening boat ride after the Puerto Suelo beach bonfire, we conclude through Manialtepec's glowing bioluminescent water.

How is it different from the 3-hour night bioluminescence tour?

The night tour focuses purely on after-dark swimming (3 hours); this 5-hour option adds 13 km of daytime mangrove birdwatching plus a sunset beach bonfire.

Is it ideal for couples and families?

Absolutely — calm lagoon cruising, sunset beach walking, and bonfire marshmallows make it a favorite for couples and families alike.

What if it is near the Full Moon?

Daytime wildlife, golden sunset views, and the beach bonfire are incredible every night; message us on WhatsApp if peak bioluminescence contrast is your top priority.

Should I wear a light layer for evening?

Yes, bringing a light jacket keeps you cozy on the open boat ride back after dark.
