What is Manialtepec's Bioluminescence?

The bioluminescence in Manialtepec Lagoon is a natural wonder where microscopic unicellular organisms called dinoflagellates (Pyrodinium bahamense) emit blue-green flashes of light when stimulated by water movement.

When you swim, move your hand, or an oar cuts through the surface, the luciferase enzyme inside each microscopic organism oxidizes luciferin to produce cold light photons. It feels like swimming inside a liquid starry sky — and Manialtepec Lagoon, located just 14 km from Puerto Escondido, Oaxaca, is one of the brightest natural sanctuaries on the planet to experience it.

When is the Best Time to See the Bioluminescence?

Bioluminescence occurs year-round in Manialtepec Lagoon, with seasonal intensity variations:

📅 Seasonal Glow Calendar

  • July to November (Rainy Season): MAXIMUM intensity. The mixing of fresh river water with Pacific saltwater creates an explosive bloom of dinoflagellates.
  • December to June (Dry Season): Moderate-to-good glow. Waters are exceptionally calm and clear.

🌙 Critical Factor: The Moon Phase

The moon phase is the single most critical factor for the visual quality of your experience:

  • New Moon: Completely dark sky. 100% glow intensity. You can see fish swimming like glowing meteors beneath the surface.
  • Waxing / Waning Crescent: Very good, scheduled before moonrise or after moonset.
  • Full Moon: AVOID. Bright moonlight significantly diminishes the contrast of the glow.

Local Tip: Message us on WhatsApp and we will provide the exact New Moon dates to schedule your tour at the ideal time.

What Should You Wear & Bring?

  • Swimsuit and towel.
  • Comfortable dark clothes that can get wet.
  • Water shoes or secure strap sandals.
  • DO NOT wear chemical repellents or sunscreens. Compounds like DEET are lethal toxins to dinoflagellates. Please rinse off lotions before the tour.
  • No flash photography — it ruins night vision for fellow travelers.

Frequently Asked Questions about Bioluminescence

¿Cuál es la mejor época para ver la bioluminiscencia en Manialtepec?

Bioluminescence is present year-round, peaking from July to November during rainy season. Booking around the New Moon ensures maximum darkness and vivid glow.

¿Se puede nadar en la bioluminiscencia?

Yes, swimming in the glowing water is the highlight of the experience. Every movement triggers blue-green sparkles around your body. It is completely safe for all ages.

¿Cuánto dura el tour de bioluminiscencia?

The complete tour takes approximately 3 hours, including hotel roundtrip transfer, boat navigation, and swimming time.

¿Es seguro para niños y adultos mayores?

Yes, it is a safe, family-friendly tour. Life jackets are provided for all sizes and boats are navigated by certified local captains.

What should I wear and bring?

Wear a swimsuit, bring a towel and comfortable clothes. Crucial: Do NOT wear chemical repellents or sunscreens as they harm the microorganisms.

¿Cómo afecta la luna a la bioluminiscencia?

The moon phase is essential. During New Moon, the glow is 100% visible. On Full Moon nights, ambient moonlight significantly reduces visibility.
