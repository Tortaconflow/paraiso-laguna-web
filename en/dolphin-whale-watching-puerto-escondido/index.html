Ethical Marine Ecotourism on the Oaxaca Coast

The warm and nutrient-rich Pacific currents off Puerto Escondido, Oaxaca create a thriving haven for marine life. Our dolphin and whale watching tour allows you to experience wild marine mammals thriving freely in their natural open-ocean habitat.

We depart into the open ocean right at sunrise. Early golden morning light reflecting on calm waters creates ideal photography conditions as we encounter mega-pods of wild dolphins often numbering more than a hundred individuals.

Marine Species You Will Encounter

Across our four-hour ocean expedition led by certified captains with decades of Pacific navigation experience, we regularly encounter:

  • Spinner Dolphins: World-famous for their high-flying aerial corkscrews and energetic leaps.
  • Bottlenose Dolphins: Intelligent and playful, often riding the boat's bow wave.
  • Sea Turtles: Olive Ridley and Pacific Green turtles resting peacefully on the ocean surface.
  • Mobula Rays: Spectacular schooling rays leaping feet above the surface.

🐋 Humpback Whale Season (December to March)

From mid-December through late March, the Oaxaca Coast welcomes the annual migration of the humpback whale (Megaptera novaeangliae). Reaching up to 16 meters (52 feet) in length, these gentle giants travel from northern feeding waters to mate and calve in our warm Pacific waters.

During winter departures, you have a strong chance of witnessing dramatic blows, tail slaps, and full multi-ton aerial breaches offshore.

Safety & Responsible Wildlife Navigation

We prioritize both guest safety and zero-harassment marine wildlife standards following official ecotourism protocols:

  • Respectful Distances: We never chase or block dolphin pods or mother-and-calf whales, idling engines smoothly at safe viewing distances.
  • Licensed Local Captains: All crew hold official maritime licenses and safety rescue training.
  • Certified Gear: Mandatory fitted life jackets and sanitized snorkel gear (when sea conditions allow a brief swim) are provided on board.

Morning Ocean Safari Itinerary

  1. 6:30 AM — Direct pickup from your hotel in Puerto Escondido.
  2. 6:50 AM — Arrival at Bahía Principal dock and crew safety orientation.
  3. 7:00 AM — Departure into open waters as the sun rises over the Sierra Madre coastline.
  4. 7:30 AM to 9:30 AM — Searching for and observing resident wild dolphin pods leaping beside the bow.
  5. 9:30 AM to 10:30 AM — Watching sea turtles, mobula rays, and migrating humpback whales (winter season), plus optional calm-water swim at the captain's discretion.
  6. 10:45 AM — Coastal return cruising past Zicatela, Carrizalillo, and Puerto Angelito bays.
  7. 11:00 AM — Ground drop-off back at your hotel.

Frequently Asked Questions About Dolphin & Whale Watching

What is the best time of year to see dolphins in Puerto Escondido?

Wild dolphins live year-round off the Oaxaca coast, giving sunrise departures a sighting success rate above 90% in every season.

When is humpback whale season in Oaxaca?

The official humpback whale migration runs from mid-December through late March, when mothers and calves rest in warm coastal Pacific waters.

Is the open-ocean boat tour safe?

Yes. Our boats are built for coastal Pacific navigation and piloted by licensed local captains, with mandatory certified life jackets for every guest.

What if I am prone to seasickness?

Taking a preventive motion-sickness tablet 30 minutes before boarding helps greatly. Departing at 7:00 AM also ensures the calmest ocean conditions of the day.

Can young children join the dolphin watching tour?

Yes, children from age 2 upward are welcome with their parents and love watching dolphins leap beside the boat.

What should I wear and bring on the boat?

Wear a swimsuit under quick-dry clothes that can get splashed, plus a towel, sunglasses, secure hat, and a light windbreaker for the crisp early morning breeze.
