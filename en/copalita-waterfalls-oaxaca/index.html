Copalita Magic Waterfalls: Mountain Adventure from Puerto Escondido

Escape coastal humidity on a scenic ascent into Oaxaca's Sierra Sur mountains. Tucked near the Chatino village of San Juan Lachao, emerald waterfalls cascade across boulders into refreshing natural forest pools.

A Lush Mountain Oasis in Oaxaca's Sierra Sur

Trading ocean waves for cool mountain air, the Copalita Magic Waterfalls tour ascends past coffee plantations and mist-draped ridge overlooks toward San Juan Lachao.

Fed by pure mountain streams, a string of cascading falls fills bright green pools surrounded by giant ferns, mossy rocks, and songbirds far from busy beach towns.

What Is Included?

  • Roundtrip transport from your hotel or meeting point in Puerto Escondido.
  • Community trail entry fees supporting local landowners and upkeep.
  • Certified bilingual guide leading safely along the trails and best swimming spots.
  • Traveler insurance included throughout the day.

🌿 Highlights of the Waterfalls Excursion

  • Multiple natural pools ranging from waist-deep wades to cool plunge spots.
  • Natural shade and crisp mountain temperatures beneath tall jungle canopy.
  • Optional homestyle Chatino meal stop at traditional village kitchens along the route.

Full-Day Itinerary (7 Hours)

  1. 9:00 AM — Hotel pickup in Puerto Escondido and scenic mountain drive inland.
  2. 10:30 AM — Arrival at San Juan Lachao trailhead and guided forest walk.
  3. 11:30 AM to 2:00 PM — Waterfall arrival, swimming in emerald pools, and relaxing in nature.
  4. 2:15 PM — Optional regional food break on the return route (cash only).
  5. 4:00 PM — Drop-off back at your hotel in Puerto Escondido.

What to Bring

  • Swimsuit worn underneath comfortable quick-dry clothes and a towel.
  • Closed-toe water shoes or grip sneakers — wet river rocks can be slippery.
  • Dry change of clothes for the return drive, biodegradable bug spray, and cash for local meals.

Frequently Asked Questions About Copalita Waterfalls

How strenuous is the hike to Copalita Waterfalls?

The trail is moderate with natural stone steps and uneven forest paths. Guests with everyday mobility complete it comfortably at a steady pace.

Can you swim in the waterfalls year-round?

Yes. Rainy season (June–October) brings dramatic full flow, while dry season (November–May) delivers extra clear tranquil emerald pools.

Are children welcome on this trip?

Active kids aged 6+ accustomed to walking nature trails enjoy it with parental supervision and sturdy grip shoes.

Is lunch included?

Meals are excluded so you can choose freely at small local kitchens on the mountain route (please carry Mexican pesos).

What footwear works best?

Sturdy sneakers or closed sport water sandals with tread. Loose flip-flops are unsafe on damp boulders.
