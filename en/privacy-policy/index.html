Privacy Policy & Eco-Tour Booking Terms

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Data Controller

Paraíso Laguna, located at Laguna de Manialtepec, Km 16 Carr. Federal 200, Puerto Escondido, Oaxaca, Mexico, C.P. 71980, is responsible for protecting the personal data you share with us, in accordance with Mexico's Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP).

For any privacy inquiry, please write to reilyvica@gmail.com or message us via WhatsApp at +52 954 161 1334.

2. Information We Collect

This website does not collect credit card numbers or process automated online payments. When you fill out the quote request form on our home page, your entered details are formatted locally on your device to open a direct WhatsApp message:

  • Your name.
  • Desired eco-tour experience, group size, and travel date.
  • Optional notes such as hotel pickup details or special questions.

3. Purpose of Processing

Information shared through WhatsApp or email is used strictly to:

  • Confirm real-time tour availability and provide direct operator group quotes.
  • Coordinate roundtrip transport, certified bilingual naturalist guides, safety equipment, and traveler insurance.
  • Notify guests promptly regarding tidal conditions, lunar brightness windows, or weather-related rescheduling.

4. Web Analytics & Cookies (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (G-6N2D25Z3EW) to measure aggregated site traffic, language selection, and WhatsApp inquiry clicks so we can improve usability and tour information. You may disable or limit cookies at any time via your browser preferences.

5. Third-Party Platforms & ARCO Rights

When you open a chat in WhatsApp (WhatsApp LLC / Meta) or interact with Google Maps embeds, those third-party services apply their respective privacy policies. Paraíso Laguna never sells, rents, or trades your data for third-party advertising.

You retain full rights of Access, Rectification, Cancellation, and Opposition (ARCO rights) regarding your personal information and may exercise them anytime by emailing reilyvica@gmail.com.

6. Outdoor Operation & Weather Rescheduling

Direct WhatsApp quotes confirm tentative space subject to itinerary details. Because all excursions operate in living coastal habitats (Manialtepec Lagoon, the Pacific Ocean, and Chacahua National Park), tours may be rescheduled free of charge if adverse weather or coastal port authority advisories make navigation unsafe.
