Four Iconic Stops Along Oaxaca's Emerald Coast

Just over an hour southeast along Highway 200 lies Oaxaca's laid-back coastal triangle. Instead of juggling hot rural buses, our guided day excursion connects La Ventanilla, Zipolite, bohemian Mazunte, and the sweeping headland of Punta Cometa in seamless comfort.

Day Tour Highlights

  • La Ventanilla Eco-Reserve: Boat ride and island walk spotting basking crocodiles, iguanas, rescued spider monkeys, and white-tailed deer cared for by local families.
  • Zipolite & Mazunte Pueblo Mágico: Browse natural artisan skincare workshops, stroll oceanfront streets, and enjoy Oaxacan chocolate & mezcal tasting stops.
  • Sunset at Punta Cometa: Easy cliffside walk to Mexico's southern Pacific peninsula tip for an unobstructed sunset horizon.

🌅 Why Book a Guided Day Trip

  • Stress-free door-to-door A/C van pickup and night return to Puerto Escondido.
  • Timed smoothly so you reach Punta Cometa before golden hour without rushing.
  • Local insights into Zapotec-coast communities and eco-conservation projects.

Full-Day Itinerary (9 Hours)

  1. 9:00 AM — Hotel pickup in Puerto Escondido traveling southeast along the coast.
  2. 10:15 AM — La Ventanilla sanctuary canoe & wildlife trail tour.
  3. 12:15 PM — Scenic coastal drive past Zipolite into charming Mazunte village.
  4. 2:00 PM — Free oceanfront lunch break plus artisanal cacao/mezcal tasting.
  5. 5:00 PM — Sunset walk out to Punta Cometa sea cliffs overlooking the Pacific.
  6. 6:30 PM — Return van transfer back to your Puerto Escondido hotel (~7:30 PM drop-off).

What to Pack

  • Comfortable walking shoes or secure sandals for Punta Cometa's coastal trail.
  • Sunglasses, wide sun hat, reef-safe sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle.
  • Cash (Mexican pesos) for lunch, crafts, and community sanctuary fees.

Frequently Asked Questions About Mazunte & Punta Cometa

How steep is the walk to Punta Cometa?

It is a scenic 15–20 minute dirt-and-step coastal path that guests of nearly all fitness levels manage easily.

What wildlife lives in La Ventanilla?

White-tailed deer, rescued spider monkeys, green iguanas, herons, cormorants, and wild American crocodiles.

Is lunch included in the tour?

Lunch is ordered on your own at beachfront eateries in Mazunte so you choose what you crave; an artisanal cacao/mezcal tasting stop is featured.

What time do we return to Puerto Escondido?

Right after sunset (~6:30 PM departure), dropping you safely back at your hotel around 7:30–8:00 PM.

Can families with kids join this excursion?

Yes — kids love spotting wildlife in Ventanilla and watching the dramatic ocean waves from Punta Cometa.
