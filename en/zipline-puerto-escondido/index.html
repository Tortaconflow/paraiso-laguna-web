Canopy Thrills Deep in the Chatino Mountains

Built for travelers craving genuine outdoor adrenaline beyond the beach, our Extreme Zipline Adventure takes you inland to San Juan Lachao. Before clipping into the cables, you hike a forested mountain path ending at a cold freshwater waterfall pool.

After drying off, certified local instructors fit your helmet, harness, and pulley gear so you can fly smoothly across five consecutive canyon lines.

What Is Included?

  • Five-zipline canopy circuit spanning steep mountain valleys.
  • Certified safety gear: harness, helmet, double safety carabiners, and assisted braking.
  • Bottled water & roundtrip transport from Puerto Escondido.
  • Traveler insurance included all day.

🧗 Safety Standards & Requirements

  • Full hands-on safety orientation prior to your first platform launch.
  • Sturdy closed-toe athletic sneakers or hiking shoes are strictly mandatory.
  • Beginner-friendly: instructors handle clipping and landing reception at every line.

Full-Day Itinerary (7 Hours)

  1. 9:00 AM — Hotel pickup in Puerto Escondido for scenic mountain transit.
  2. 10:30 AM — Jungle trail hike to the hidden waterfall for refreshing swimming time.
  3. 12:15 PM — Safety fitting and high-flying ride across all 5 canyon ziplines.
  4. 2:15 PM — Optional homestyle mountain meal break (cash recommended).
  5. 4:00 PM — Drop-off return to Puerto Escondido.

What to Wear

  • Comfortable sporty shorts or leggings that fit cleanly under a climbing harness.
  • Closed-toe sneakers required (no open sandals or slip-on shoes).
  • Hair tie for long hair, swimsuit underneath, small towel, and sunglasses with retainer strap.

Frequently Asked Questions About Extreme Ziplining

Is previous zipline experience needed?

None at all. Certified instructors give a clear briefing and manage safety clips and braking on every platform.

Why are closed sneakers mandatory?

Platform steps, forest trails, and mid-air launches require snug lace-up grip footwear; open sandals are not allowed.

Can kids or teens join the zipline circuit?

Youth aged 8+ who meet harness sizing usually participate with a parent; message us on WhatsApp with guest weights/heights.

How long do the hike, waterfall, and ziplines take?

About 3 active hours in the sierra, making a 7-hour roundtrip excursion from Puerto Escondido.

Can I carry my phone or action camera?

Only with a secure chest mount or wrist lanyard so devices stay safe above the canyon.
